Our Mission

The National Junior College Athletic Association (NJCAA) is the governing body of two-year college athletics, promoting the educational ideals and physical well-being of student-athletes.

Founded in 1938, the NJCAA provides opportunities for student-athletes to compete at the highest level while pursuing academic excellence. We believe in the power of athletics to transform lives and open doors to future success.

OUR VALUES

Academic Excellence

We prioritize education and support student-athletes in achieving their academic goals alongside their athletic pursuits.

Competitive Excellence

We provide high-quality athletic competition that prepares student-athletes for success at the next level.

Integrity

We uphold the highest standards of ethical conduct, fair play, and sportsmanship in all we do.

Opportunity

We create pathways for student-athletes to pursue their dreams, regardless of background or circumstance.
